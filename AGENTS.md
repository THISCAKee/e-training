## Website design preference

For future website design and visual updates in this project, use this palette as the default: navy `#003049` for primary text and navigation, red `#D62828` for destructive or error states, orange `#F77F00` for actions and accents, and yellow `#FCBF49` for highlights. Keep the overall interface clean with white or pale blue-gray surfaces, restrained shadows, and accessible text contrast; use the bright colors sparingly rather than giving all four equal weight.
